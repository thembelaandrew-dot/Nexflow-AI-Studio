# NexaFlow AI - Marketing & Design Prompts

This document contains a comprehensive list of prompts, copy, and design instructions to create your marketing assets. Since AI image generators struggle with exact text, the best approach is to generate the **backgrounds and visual assets** using the AI prompts below, and then add the **Text Copy** using a tool like Canva, Figma, or Photoshop.

---

## 🎨 Core Brand Aesthetic (Use in all design tools)
*   **Primary Colors:** Deep Space Black (`#02050c`), Electric Blue (`#3b82f6`), Neon Cyan (`#06b6d4`).
*   **Style:** Modern, sleek, glassmorphism, premium, high-tech, minimalist.
*   **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, or Montserrat (Bold for headers, regular for body).
